🥊 PHASE 2: MULTI-CLASS FINE-GRAINED CRIME ACTION RECOGNITION (UCF-Crime)
========================================================================================
Triggered only when Phase 1 flags an active crime event (Threat P > 0.50).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Experiment / Architecture               Classes      Train Acc   Val Acc    Top-1 Test   Top-3 Test   Notes / Findings
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SlowFast Net (14 Classes)            14 Classes   54.0%       51.58%     49.47%       --           Suffered from 19:1 NormalVideos majority class bias.
2. Naive VideoViT (13 Classes)          13 Classes   97.75%      33.16%     28.91%       --           Overfit to static room wallpapers (unfrozen backbone).
3. Regularized VideoViT (Frozen Spatial) 13 Classes   96.38%      29.12%     22.61%       47.35%       Confused by identical physical actions (Fight vs Assault).
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🔍 Root Cause of 13-Class Standstill:
In UCF-Crime, 13 classes are legal penal code labels, NOT visually distinct actions:
- Physical Conflict: "Fighting", "Assault", "Abuse", and "Shooting" share identical wrestling/striking kinematics.
- Property Theft: "Burglary", "Stealing", "Robbery", and "Shoplifting" share identical hand-reaching/running kinematics.

========================================================================================
🚀 PHASE 2 OPTIMIZATION: 4 HIERARCHICAL META-ACTION CLUSTERS
========================================================================================
Grouping into physically distinct kinematic clusters:
  Group 1: PHYSICAL VIOLENCE & ASSAULT    (Fighting, Assault, Abuse, Shooting)
  Group 2: THEFT, ROBBERY & BREAK-IN      (Robbery, Burglary, Stealing, Shoplifting)
  Group 3: DISASTER, HAZARD & ARSON       (Arson, Explosion, Road Accidents)
  Group 4: VANDALISM & PUBLIC DISTURBANCE (Vandalism, Arrest)
